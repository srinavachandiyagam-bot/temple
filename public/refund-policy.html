Last updated: 16 September 2026

Important: This website is for event participation registration only. No physical products are sold and no shipping of goods occurs. Therefore there is no product return or exchange process. This Refund Policy governs participation payments only. For shipping, see Shipping Policy.

1. Cancellation of Participation by Participant

If you wish to cancel your participation after registering and paying, please contact the temple committee as soon as possible using the contact details in Section 9. Cancellation requests are reviewed case by case. Whether a refund is applicable depends on timing, event arrangements already made, and administrative costs incurred.

Because sankalpam arrangements and temple logistics are organised in advance, cancellations close to the event date may not be eligible for refund. Any approved refund will be processed to the original payment method only.

2. Refund Eligibility

Refunds are considered in the following situations:

  • Duplicate payment: If you were charged twice for the same registration (e.g. double click, network retry), the duplicate amount is eligible for refund after verification.
  • Payment debited but registration not confirmed: If your bank/UPI shows a debit but the website shows payment failed or pending and no confirmation was received, we will verify with the payment gateway and refund if the payment is confirmed as unsuccessful on our records.
  • Event cancelled or postponed by organiser: If the Yagam is cancelled or postponed by the temple committee (see Section 6), the policy in that section applies.
  • Technical error: Proven technical errors where the payment gateway confirms failure but amount was debited.

Refunds are not applicable for change of mind after successful confirmation where the sankalpam has been included, or for inability to attend the event personally (you may be considered as having participated via sankalpam).

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact us within a reasonable time and provide:

  • Primary participant name and mobile number used for registration
  • Registration ID / order ID if available (shown after payment)
  • Date and amount of payment, payment method (UPI / card / net banking)
  • Bank/UPI reference or transaction ID (from your bank statement or gateway receipt)
  • Reason for request

Contact: Phone 9150232419 / 7539953653 / 6381606039 or WhatsApp 9150232419. You may also visit Contact section on the website.

We will verify the transaction with our records and the payment gateway before confirming eligibility.

4. Payment Failure & Amount Debited but Registration Not Confirmed

If your account was debited but the registration page showed “Payment failed” or remains pending:

  • Wait for a short time – some banks show a temporary debit that auto-reverses.
  • Check your bank/UPI app for the final status after 30 minutes.
  • If the debit is confirmed, contact us with the transaction reference. We will cross-check with the gateway’s order status API / webhook logs.
  • If the gateway confirms the payment as failed/not captured, we will initiate a refund for that transaction.

5. Duplicate Payment

If you paid twice for the same mobile number / same registration attempt, please share both transaction IDs. After confirming two successful debits for essentially one registration, one payment will be refunded in full (the duplicate). The original registration remains valid.

6. Event Cancellation or Postponement by Organiser

If the temple committee cancels the Yagam due to unavoidable circumstances (e.g. natural calamity, government order, temple emergency):

  • We will notify registered participants via the contact details provided (SMS / WhatsApp / call).
  • Where the event is cancelled outright and not rescheduled, paid participants will be offered a refund of the participation amount (₹1,000 per registration) to the original payment method.
  • If postponed to a new date, your registration will remain valid for the new date. You may request a refund if you cannot attend on the postponed date; please contact us within the period communicated in the postponement notice.

7. Refund Processing

Procedure after approval:

  • Refunds are initiated to the original payment method (same UPI, card, wallet or bank account) via the payment gateway’s refund API.
  • Internal processing: typically 5–7 working days after approval and verification.
  • Bank/gateway credit: after we initiate, the acquiring bank and your issuing bank/UPI provider take an additional 5–10 working days to credit your account, depending on the method.
  • You will be informed of the refund reference once initiated. Delays beyond our initiation are governed by banking networks and are not within the temple committee’s control.

Configurable note: The timelines above (“5–7 working days + 5–10 days bank”) are standard estimates. To change them, edit this paragraph. Do not promise instant refunds; always allow for gateway/bank processing.

8. Payment Gateway / Bank Processing Delays

Sometimes the gateway shows “pending” while the bank has debited, or vice versa. We rely on the gateway’s webhook and order-status verification. Please allow up to 24–48 hours for settlement. If after that the status is still pending and your bank confirms a debit, contact us for manual verification using the “Sync” check in our admin (for internal use). We will update you once verified.
