3. Event Information

  • Event: Nava Chandi Yagam – a special Vedic worship dedicated to Goddess Chandi for family sankalpam, well-being, health, prosperity and peace.
  • Date: 18 October 2026
  • Time: 7:00 AM

4. Participant Registration

The website provides an online registration form for devotees to register themselves and family members for the sankalpam. To register you will be asked to provide:

  • Primary participant name and 10-digit mobile number (required)
  • Rasi and Natchathiram (as required by the form)
  • Gothram, email, address (optional unless marked required)
  • Family member details – name, Rasi, Natchathiram, Gothram for up to 4 additional members

Registration is complete only after successful payment confirmation where a payment is required. The organiser may set limits on maximum family members or required fields via the admin settings.

5. Accuracy of Participant Information

You are responsible for ensuring that all information you provide – including names, mobile number, email, Rasi, Natchathiram, Gothram and family details – is accurate and complete. Inaccurate sankalpam details may affect the prayer dedication. The temple committee will use the details exactly as submitted for sankalpam purposes. Please verify before payment.
