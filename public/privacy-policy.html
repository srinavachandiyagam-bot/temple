Privacy Policy

Nava Chandi Yagam • 18 October 2026 • 7:00 AM • navachandiyagam.online
Last updated: 16 September 2026

Summary: This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you register for the Nava Chandi Yagam, how we use it, and how we protect it. The website collects only the information shown on the registration form plus standard technical logs; payments are handled by a PCI-compliant third-party gateway.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you directly provide through the registration form and information collected automatically when you use the website.

A. Registration Information (provided by you)

Based on the actual registration form on navachandiyagam.online, we may collect:

We do not collect date of birth/age unless you enter it in the address or additional notes field. We collect Rasi, Natchathiram and Gothram specifically for the sankalpam, as per Hindu tradition.

B. Automatically collected information

  • IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and time of visit (standard server logs).
  • Cookies or local storage for language preference (Tamil/English) and login session for the admin panel only. No tracking cookies for advertising.

2. How We Use Information

  • To register you and your family members for the Nava Chandi Yagam sankalpam on 18 October 2026.
  • To confirm registration and communicate event details, venue guidance and updates via your mobile number, WhatsApp, SMS, email (if provided) or phone.
  • To verify and reconcile participation payments through the payment gateway.
  • To respond to enquiries, refund requests, and support messages.
  • To maintain internal records for temple administration and audit.
  • To improve website functionality and security (logs and analytics are aggregated where possible).

3. Registration Information

Registration details are stored in our database (SQLite or PostgreSQL, depending on deployment) hosted on our server. Access is limited to authorised temple committee administrators via the protected /admin panel. Registration data may be exported as CSV for administrative planning (seating, sankalpam list, prasadam counts). We retain the family structure you submit exactly as entered.

4. Payment Information

We do not store your full payment instrument details on our servers:

  • Card numbers, UPI PIN, CVV, card PIN, banking passwords and OTPs are entered only on the payment gateway’s secure checkout page (Cashfree / PhonePe).
  • We store only the payment metadata returned by the gateway: payment status (paid/pending/failed), order ID (e.g. NCY_...), cashfree_order_id / payment ID, payment method, payment time, bank reference and payment message.
  • The participation amount (₹999) and currency (INR) are recorded with the registration.

5. Payment Gateway / PhonePe / Cashfree

When you click “Proceed to Payment”, you are redirected to the gateway’s hosted checkout. The gateway is a PCI-DSS compliant third party that independently handles encryption and authorisation. Your relationship with the gateway for the transaction is also governed by the gateway’s privacy policy and terms. We receive only a success/failure notification and reference IDs, not your authentication secrets.

We never write PhonePe Merchant ID, secret keys or Cashfree client secret to any public page.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Payment gateway (Cashfree / PhonePe) for payment processing.
  • Google Maps for venue location links.
  • WhatsApp for contact via wa.me links.
  • Cashfree SDK (sdk.cashfree.com) loaded on the registration page for checkout.
  • Hosting provider (e.g. Render/Hostinger) for infrastructure logs.

Clicking those links or completing payment will share only the necessary data with that third party. We do not sell your data to advertisers.

7. Data Sharing

We share personal data only as necessary to operate the Yagam:

  • With the temple committee and priests for sankalpam and event logistics.
  • With the payment gateway to initiate and verify your transaction.
  • With hosting / database providers who store data on our behalf under confidentiality.
  • If required by law, court order or lawful government request.

We do not share Rasi, Gothram or family data for marketing.

8. Data Security

  • All public traffic should be served over HTTPS (TLS). In production, the hosting provider issues the certificate.
  • Admin access is protected by password and token-based authentication.
  • Database access is restricted to the server environment.
  • We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures, but no internet transmission is 100% secure. Please keep your registration ID and payment reference confidential.

We do not claim any specific certification (e.g. ISO 27001, SOC 2) unless the hosting / gateway provider displays it. Payment security certifications belong to the gateway, not to this temple website.

9. Data Retention

  • Registration records are retained for as long as needed for event administration, accounting and audit, typically until the Yagam is completed and statutory retention periods expire.
  • Server logs may be retained for a shorter period for security and debugging.
  • You may request deletion or correction by contacting us (see Section 12). Deletion will be honoured subject to legal and accounting obligations.

10. User Rights

  • Access and correction: you may ask to see what registration data we hold for your mobile number and request corrections.
  • Withdrawal: you may ask to withdraw your registration (see Refund Policy for payment implications).
  • Deletion: you may request deletion where not required for legal compliance.
  • To exercise rights, contact us with your name, mobile number and registration ID/order ID if available.

11. Consent

By submitting the registration form and ticking the consent checkbox (where shown), you consent to collection and use of your information as described here. You also consent to being contacted on the mobile number / email provided for event and payment updates via call, SMS, WhatsApp or email.
